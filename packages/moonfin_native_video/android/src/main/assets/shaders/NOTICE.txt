Upscaling shader used by Lambent's Auto HDR on Android TV (LambentHdrStage / LambentHookShader)

ravu-lite-ar-r3.hook is the mpv user shader as published, unmodified. The app reads and compiles it at run time, so it contains none
of its code, and you may replace this file with your own build of the same or a modified shader.
