Upscaling shaders used by Lambent's Auto HDR on the Apple TV (Playback/Lambent/LambentPrescaler.swift)

Each <name>.metal / <name>.json pair here is a mechanical translation, by Lambent's hook2native.py, of the mpv user shader of the
same name in source/ (unmodified). The app compiles the .metal file at run time, so it contains none of this code, and you may
replace any pair with your own translation of the same or a modified shader.
